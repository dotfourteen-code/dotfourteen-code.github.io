Shimmo: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026. Version 1.

1. Who we are

Shimmo is a single-player puzzle game for iPhone published by Nurzhan Kerimbekov (developer, "we"). Contact for privacy questions: nurzhan.dev@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • On your device only: game progress, settings, language, and whether you bought "Remove ads". The game has no account, login or sign-up. We run no servers that receive your progress.
  • Analytics: the game does not send analytics or crash reports to us or to an analytics service.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): to show ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data (for example, which ads were shown or watched), plus crash and performance data of the SDK. This is used to deliver, measure and secure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Consent: Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) stores your consent choice. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in other regions where required, it asks before personalised ads.
  • Purchases: payment is handled by Apple. We never see your card details. The game checks with the App Store whether "Remove ads" is owned (the Unity In-App Purchasing component talks to the App Store for this).

3. How we use it

To run the game, show ads and rewarded videos, process and restore purchases, and comply with the law. We do not sell your data and we do not build profiles ourselves.

4. Tracking and your choices

  • iOS tracking prompt: after you play a few levels, iOS asks whether Shimmo may track you across other apps and websites. You can say no and still play everything; ads are then not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ad consent: in the game, Settings > "Ad consent settings" reopens the consent form where it is required.
  • Remove ads: the one-time purchase turns off banners and ads between levels. Optional rewarded videos stay available by your choice.

5. Who receives data

Google (AdMob, UMP) and advertisers and ad partners that take part in Google's ad auctions; Apple (App Store purchases and the tracking prompt); Unity Technologies (game engine and purchasing component). They act under their own policies. Google: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner sites. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

6. Retention and deletion

Progress stays on your device until you delete the game or use Settings > "Delete my data", which erases progress and settings from the device. "Remove ads" is kept by Apple and can be restored with Settings > "Restore purchases". Data held by Google and Apple is kept under their policies. For other requests, write to the email above.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, California and other US states) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We hold no profile about you ourselves, so most requests are met through the in-game settings and iOS settings above; write to us for anything else. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

Shimmo is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Ad requests are not marked as child-directed. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, write to us.

9. Security

Progress is stored in the game's private storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes

If we make material changes we will update this page and the effective date, and show a notice in the game where required.
