Shimmo: Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to nurzhan.dev@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the game version (Settings > bottom of the screen).

Common questions

I bought "Remove ads" but ads are back. Open Settings > "Restore purchases" with the same Apple ID. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not to the game.

How do I get a refund? Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple: support.apple.com/118223.

How do I change my ad choices? In the game: Settings > "Ad consent settings" (where required). On iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How do I delete my data? Settings > "Delete my data" erases progress and settings on the device. We keep no account or server data about you.

I am stuck on a level. Restarting and undo are free. After a few failed tries the game may offer an optional video to skip the level.
